## Before you start

**Ton parcours** (your journey) is the web page an athlete sees after a first session in your gym without a gym subscription. It is the athlete side of the **Adhérents** tab in **Prospects** (Communauté → Prospects), which is for the owner and co-owner only. The athlete opens it with their AthleX account, once signed in. Trial visitors without an account (**Essais** tab) have no access to it. On-screen labels are in French, so they are quoted as they appear.

## Steps: how the athlete gets there

1. A registered member with no active gym subscription is checked in at their first session, in **Horaires**. An automatic task then creates their card in **Essai réalisé** (session done) in the **Adhérents** tab.
2. The same task reminds them by app notification and by e-mail, once per step: shortly after the session (« Comment était ta séance chez … ? », button **Laisser mon avis**), the next day (« On se voit pour la suite ? », **Réserver un créneau**), then three days later (« Prêt à te lancer chez … ? », **Voir les offres**). Each reminder only goes out if the card has not moved past that step yet.
3. Every e-mail button leads to **Ton parcours**. No link in the Manager or in the athlete space leads there: those reminders are the only way in.

## Steps: what they do, what you see

1. **Their feedback.** Their card asks « Comment était ta séance ? »: 1 to 5 stars, « Un mot sur ton ressenti (optionnel) », then **Envoyer mon avis**. Your card moves to **Feedback reçu**, with their stars and comment. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/suivi-apres-l-essai/1.png" alt="Ton parcours page: gym card with “Comment était ta séance ?”, four stars ticked and the Envoyer mon avis button" />
2. **Their meeting.** Open slots in the **Créneaux RDV** tab (**Ouvrir un créneau**, then **Ajouter le créneau**). After their feedback, they see « Réserve un moment avec la box »: your upcoming slots, the coach (« avec … ») and **Réserver**, or **Complet** (full). As soon as they book, your card moves to **RDV pris** and they read « Ton RDV est réservé ». <Screenshot src="/tutorials/suivi-apres-l-essai/2.png" alt="Ton parcours page: “Réserve un moment avec la box” with three slots, one of them Complet" />
3. **Your offer.** On their **Adhérents** card, click **Proposer une offre**, pick one in **Choisir une offre…**, then **Envoyer l'offre**: the card moves to **Offre proposée**. They see « Une offre pour toi 🎯 », the name and price, and **Voir l'offre & m'abonner**, which opens your public page. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/suivi-apres-l-essai/3.png" alt="Ton parcours page: “Une offre pour toi” with the plan, its price and the Voir l'offre & m'abonner button" />
4. **The end.** Once they have subscribed, click **Marquer converti**: they read « Bienvenue dans la communauté ! ». The **Marquer perdu** cross removes their card from **Ton parcours** and stops the reminders. Both are immediate, with no confirmation. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/suivi-apres-l-essai/4.png" alt="Adhérents tab of Prospects: cards in Essai réalisé, Feedback reçu with stars, RDV pris and Offre proposée" />

<Callout>
**Envoyer l'offre** sends neither e-mail nor notification: the athlete finds it when they open **Ton parcours**. Tell them yourself. And **Marquer converti** creates no subscription: they subscribe on your public page, or you send them an invitation.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **« Rien à suivre pour l'instant. Tente une première séance dans une box ! »** They have no card (attendance not checked yet or not yet processed by the automatic task), their card is lost, or they are signed in with another account.
- **« Aucun créneau ouvert pour l'instant. La box te recontactera. »** You have no upcoming slot → open some in **Créneaux RDV**.
- **« Réservation impossible » window: « Ce créneau est complet. »** Someone took it meanwhile → they pick another one. « Réservation impossible. » alone: the slot is past, or their membership of your gym is no longer active.
- **You do not know who booked a slot.** **Créneaux RDV** only shows how full it is (« 1/2 réservé ») → look for the card in **RDV pris**.
- **The « Voir l'offre & m'abonner » button does not show.** Your gym has no public address → **Réglages → Page publique**.
- **A trial visitor looks for this page.** Without an AthleX account they have none: they stay in the **Essais** tab.

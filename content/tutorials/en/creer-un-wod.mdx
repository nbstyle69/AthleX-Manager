## Before you start

The **Whiteboard** shows the current week, one column per day. A WOD belongs to a day and a publication time, so you can prepare it in advance without it being visible. On-screen labels are in French and quoted as they appear.

## Steps

1. On the Whiteboard, move to the right week with the arrows or **Aller à une date**, then click **Nouveau WOD** (or click straight into an empty day cell). <Screenshot src="/tutorials/creer-un-wod/1.png" alt="Weekly Whiteboard with the new WOD button" />
2. Fill in the header: **Titre** (for example "Fran", "Hybrid intervals"), **Date**, **Heure**, plus **Type** and **Block** if you use those markers. The time you enter is when the WOD becomes visible on the programmed day.
3. Under **Programme / Mouvements**, add one line per movement: reps, exercise picked from the official list, then men's and women's loads. Picking the exercise from the list is what guarantees your athletes' movement badges are counted.
4. Add session notes in **Notes Coach** (scaling, cues) and a demo **Vidéo YouTube** if useful. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/creer-un-wod/2.png" alt="WOD editor: title, movements and loads" />
5. Pick the audience under **Qui reçoit ce WOD ?**: the whole box, selected groups, or nobody yet.
6. Click **Publier**. The WOD appears in the day cell; clicking the card reopens it for editing.

## Common mistakes

- **The WOD exists but no athlete sees it.** The audience is still "Personne encore" (nobody yet) → reopen the WOD and pick the whole box or the relevant groups.
- **Cannot save with "Ces groupes".** No group is ticked and the editor shows "Coche au moins un groupe." → tick the groups, or switch back to the whole box.
- **Athlete badges do not move after the session.** The movement was typed freely instead of picked from the list → edit the line and select the catalogue exercise.
- **The edit pencil on a card is inactive.** That session comes from a Marketplace programming: its content cannot be edited, only its audience.

## Before you start

The **Whiteboard** shows the current week, one row per day; the **Colonnes** button switches to one column per day. A WOD belongs to a day, and you can prepare it in advance without it being visible. On-screen labels are in French and quoted as they appear.

## Steps

1. On the Whiteboard, move to the right week with the arrows or **Aller à une date** (click the week's dates), then click **Ajouter** on the day you want, or straight into an empty day. **Nouveau WOD** also opens the editor, but dated today: change the **Date** then. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/creer-un-wod/1.png" alt="Weekly Whiteboard with the new WOD button" />
2. Fill in **Date**, **Block** and **Type** if you use those markers, then the **Titre** (for example "Fran", "Hybrid intervals"). To make it appear only at a given time, pick **Programmer** under **Publier** further down and set the **Heure**: the WOD becomes visible at that time on the programmed day. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/creer-un-wod/2.png" alt="Top of the Créer un WOD dialog: date, block, type and title" />
3. Under **Programme / Mouvements**, click **+ Ajouter un mouvement**, then add one line per movement: reps, exercise picked from the official list, then ♂ men's and ♀ women's loads when the exercise is loaded. Picking the exercise from the list is what guarantees your athletes' movement badges are counted. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/creer-un-wod/3.png" alt="Program / Movements block with two filled lines" />
4. Add session notes in **Notes Coach** (scaling, cues) and a demo **Vidéo YouTube** if useful. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/creer-un-wod/5.png" alt="Notes Coach and Vidéo YouTube blocks" />
5. At the very top of the dialog, pick the audience under **Qui reçoit ce WOD ?**: the whole box, selected groups, or nobody yet. Until you choose, **Créer le WOD** stays greyed out and the dialog shows "Choisis qui voit ce WOD". <Screenshot src="/tutorials/creer-un-wod/4.png" alt="“Who receives this WOD?” block with Toute la box selected" />
6. Leave **Publier** on (or turn it off to keep a draft), then click **Créer le WOD**. The WOD appears on the chosen day; the card's pencil (**Modifier**) reopens it.

## Common mistakes

- **The WOD exists but no athlete sees it.** The audience is "Personne encore" (nobody yet), or **Publier** is off (**Brouillon** badge) → reopen the WOD, pick the whole box or the relevant groups, and turn **Publier** on.
- **Cannot save with "Ces groupes".** No group is ticked and the editor shows "Coche au moins un groupe." → tick the groups, or switch back to the whole box.
- **Athlete badges do not move after the session.** The movement was typed freely instead of picked from the list → delete the line and add it again, picking the catalogue exercise.
- **The edit pencil on a card is inactive.** That session comes from a Marketplace programming: it does not open and its content cannot be edited. You can move or delete it; to change its audience, place the week again from **Programmation**, choosing who sees it (**Remplacer les WOD vierges**).

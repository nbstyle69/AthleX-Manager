## Before you start

The **Whiteboard** (Entraînement → Whiteboard) can receive automatically generated sessions, track by track: **Functional**, **Hybrid** and **Musculation**. The AthleX team switches this programming on for your box and picks the tracks: you cannot turn it on yourself. The owner and the coaches of the box see the same banner and can generate or regenerate.

## Read the generated week

1. Open the Whiteboard. If your box uses automatic programming, a banner reads « Semaine générée automatiquement · générée le samedi 8h · visible par les athlètes … » (generated automatically, laid on Saturday 8am, visible to athletes …) with your box's reveal time (for example « le dimanche à 18:00 », Sunday at 18:00), then the list of your active tracks.
2. Below the week navigation, tabs filter the cards: one per track present, **Box** for the WODs typed by hand, and **Tout** (all). Each tab shows its number of cards, and your choice is remembered for the box. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/whiteboard-programmation-automatique/1.png" alt="Automatically generated week banner with its two buttons, above the Functional, Hybrid, Box and Tout tabs" />
3. Each generated card carries the colour and name of its track, and an **AUTO** badge. As soon as someone touches it by hand, even just to move it to another day or unpublish it, the badge becomes **AUTO · MODIFIÉE** (edited). <Screenshot src="/tutorials/whiteboard-programmation-automatique/2.png" alt="Whiteboard card with its track badge and the AUTO · MODIFIÉE badge" />

## Generate or regenerate the week shown

1. Go to the week you want: both buttons always act on the week shown and name it.
2. Click **Générer la semaine du …** (generate the week of …) to lay the tracks that do not have their week yet. The window ticks every possible track in advance; a track already done is greyed out with « déjà générée cette semaine » (already generated this week). Click **Générer** followed by the track names.
3. To redo a track, click **Régénérer la semaine du …** (regenerate the week of …). Only tracks already generated can be ticked (« pas encore générée cette semaine », not generated yet, for the others). Untick the ones to keep, then type `REGENERER` in **Tape REGENERER pour confirmer**: the **Régénérer** button only turns on then. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/whiteboard-programmation-automatique/3.png" alt="Regenerate the week window with two tracks ticked and the word REGENERER typed" />
4. The result shows above the week, for example « … : 12 séances posées · 1 jour conservé (score ou modification) » (12 sessions laid, 1 day kept).

<Callout type="warning">
Regenerating removes and replaces the sessions of the ticked tracks. Only the days that already have a score, or that you edited by hand, are kept.
</Callout>

## Lay a Marketplace week right away

1. If the box subscribes to a Marketplace programming, one banner per subscription says what will happen: the next automatic week and the Sunday at 18:00 when it will be laid, or « Application automatique désactivée » (automatic application off) if you lay the weeks by hand.
2. Click **Appliquer maintenant** (apply now): the **Programmation** window opens with the subscription and the week due for the week shown already selected. Finish as in the tutorial "Apply a programming to the Whiteboard". <Screenshot src="/tutorials/whiteboard-programmation-automatique/4.png" alt="Marketplace subscription banner with the Appliquer maintenant button" />

## Common mistakes

- **No banner and no track tabs.** Automatic programming is not switched on for your box, or the week shown has no track card → contact the AthleX team to switch it on.
- **« Générer la semaine du … » is greyed out.** « La semaine du … est déjà générée pour toutes les pistes » (already generated for every track) → use **Régénérer** if you want to redo it.
- **The Régénérer button stays inactive.** No track is ticked (« Coche au moins une piste ») or the word is not typed (« Tape REGENERER pour activer ») → tick a track and type `REGENERER`.
- **A generated session was not replaced.** Its day has a score or an **AUTO · MODIFIÉE** card: it is kept, on purpose → edit the card by hand if it no longer suits you.
- **« La programmation automatique est éteinte pour cette box. »** (automatic programming is off) The AthleX team switched it off → contact them.
- **« Non autorisé pour cette box. »** (not allowed) Your account is neither owner nor active coach of this box.
- **Appliquer maintenant is greyed out (« Offre vide », empty offer).** The offer has no WOD yet: the publishing box has to fill it.

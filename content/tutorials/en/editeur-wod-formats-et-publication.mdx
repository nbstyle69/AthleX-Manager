## Before you start

These settings live in the **Créer un WOD** (or **Modifier le WOD**) window of the **Whiteboard** (Entraînement → Whiteboard). The owner and the coaches have access; only the **Mes offres Marketplace** section (my Marketplace offers) is reserved for the owner or co-owner.

## Choose the format

1. Open a WOD: **Ajouter** (add) on the day you want, or the pencil **Modifier** (edit) on a card.
2. In **Block**, file the session if you use these markers: **Skill GYM**, **Skill Haltéro**, **WOD**, **Pré-WOD** or **Post-WOD**.
3. In **Type (optionnel)**, pick **EMOM**: the **Intervalle EMOM** row offers **EMOM**, **E2MOM**, **E3MOM**, **E4MOM** and **E5MOM**. The text below confirms the choice, for example « Un intervalle = 2 min entre chaque départ. » (one interval = 2 min between starts). <Screenshot src="/tutorials/editeur-wod-formats-et-publication/1.png" alt="Créer un WOD window with the EMOM type and the E2MOM interval selected" />
4. For a **Tabata**, set **Travail (sec)** (work) and **Repos (sec)** (rest): 20 and 10 by default. Work is at least 5 seconds. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/editeur-wod-formats-et-publication/2.png" alt="Créer un WOD window with the Tabata type, Work 20 seconds and Rest 10 seconds" />
5. Further down, fill **Time Cap (mm:ss)**, for example `12:30`, and **Rounds (optionnel)**. A bare number in the time cap counts as minutes: `12` gives 12:00.

## Publish now or at a set time

1. The **Publier** switch (« Visible par les athlètes de la box », visible to the box's athletes) is on by default. Turn it off to keep a draft.
2. With Publier on, choose **Maintenant** (now) for a session visible as soon as it is saved, or **Programmer** (schedule) to reveal it later.
3. In **Programmer**, set **Heure :** (hours then minutes, 06:00 by default): « Le WOD sera visible à cette heure le jour programmé » (the WOD becomes visible at this time on its day). In **Colonnes** view, the card shows ⏰ and the time until it has passed.
4. **Classement** (ranking) decides what happens to scores: on, « Les scores sont classés entre membres » (scores ranked between members); off, « Scores enregistrés en historique uniquement » (history only). <Screenshot src="/tutorials/editeur-wod-formats-et-publication/3.png" alt="Bottom of the window with Publier on, Programmer chosen, time 07:00 and Classement on" />
5. Click **Créer le WOD** (or **Enregistrer**).

## Copy the WOD into a Marketplace offer (owner)

1. If your box publishes Marketplace offers, **Qui reçoit ce WOD ?** shows **Mes offres Marketplace**. Click to expand it.
2. Pick the WOD's **Date** first: the day of the offer week depends on it.
3. Click an offer, then pick its week in the list (**Semaine 1**, **Semaine 2**…). The summary at the bottom of the window adds, for example, « copié dans Force d'automne, semaine 2 » (copied into …, week 2). <Screenshot src="/tutorials/editeur-wod-formats-et-publication/4.png" alt="Mes offres Marketplace section expanded with an offer ticked and its week chosen" />
4. Save. The copy stays linked: your edits follow in the offer, and subscribed boxes keep what they have already received. Unticking the offer removes the copy.

## Common mistakes

- **Créer le WOD stays greyed out.** The **Titre**, the **Date** or the **Qui reçoit ce WOD ?** choice is missing (« Choisis qui voit ce WOD ») → fill them in.
- **Athletes do not see the session this morning.** It is on **Programmer** and the **Heure** has not passed yet → bring the time forward or choose **Maintenant**.
- **The WOD carries the Brouillon (draft) badge.** **Publier** is off → turn it on, or click the **Publier** eye on the card.
- **The interval or the Tabata settings are gone.** They are only kept for the **EMOM** or **Tabata** type → set the right type again.
- **The offers are greyed out.** « Choisis d'abord une date : la copie a besoin du jour de la semaine. » (pick a date first) → fill in the **Date**.
- **« WOD enregistré, mais tout n'a pas été posé — offre … : Accès refusé : gérant ou co-gérant de la box éditrice requis ».** (WOD saved, but not everything was set: access denied) A coach ticked an offer: the WOD is saved on the Whiteboard, the copy is not → the owner makes it.
- **Scores are not ranked.** **Classement** is off (« Scores enregistrés en historique uniquement ») → reopen the WOD and turn it on.

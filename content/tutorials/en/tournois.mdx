## Before you start

**Tournois** brings the box to life: one event, participants, a ranking. The page separates ongoing and upcoming tournaments from the history.

## Steps

1. Open **Tournois** and create a tournament. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/tournois/1.png" alt="Tournament list of the box" />
2. Fill it in: name, date, level and format. The status shown in the list then follows the life cycle of the event.
3. Open the tournament to manage its participants: box members are added from its record.
4. On the day, enter the results from the tournament record: the ranking recomputes as you go.
5. Once finished, the tournament moves to the history and the performances stay visible to athletes.

## Common mistakes

- **"Aucun tournoi." (no tournament).** Nothing has been created in this box yet → create one.
- **A member cannot be registered.** They are not an active member of the box → check their member record.
- **The ranking looks frozen.** Not all results are entered → complete the missing scores from the tournament record.
- **A past tournament clutters the list.** It moves to the history once finished → change its status rather than deleting it, so performances are kept.

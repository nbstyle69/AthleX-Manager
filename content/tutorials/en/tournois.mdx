## Before you start

**Tournois** brings the box to life: one event, participants, a ranking. The page separates **En cours & à venir** (ongoing and upcoming) from the **Historique** (closed tournaments); the **Actifs / Archivés** filter shows archived tournaments.

## Steps

1. Open **Tournois** and click **Créer un tournoi**. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/tournois/1.png" alt="Tournament list of the box" />
2. Pick the **Format du tournoi**, then fill in name, dates, level, maximum number of participants and, if needed, **Inscriptions ouvertes pendant le tournoi**. The format cannot be changed afterwards. Click **Publier** to open registrations, or **Enregistrer**. The tournament starts on its own on its start date (00:00, Paris time). <Screenshot src="/tutorials/tournois/2.png" alt="Tournament creation form" />
3. After creation you land on **Programme des WODs**: click **Ajouter un WOD** for each event. Athletes register themselves in the app; the **Participants** tile of the record lists them, and **Exclure** removes an entrant.
4. During the tournament, athletes send their scores from the app: validate them in **Scores à valider** (**Valider**, **Rejeter**, or correct the value). The **Classement** is recomputed on each validation. For a bracket, decide the matches in **Tableau du bracket** (**Décider selon les scores**, then **Round suivant**).
5. To finish, click **Terminer le tournoi** (WODs close, the tournament becomes « En révision »), validate the last scores, then **Distribuer l'ELO et clôturer**. Once closed, it moves to the **Historique** and its results stay visible.

## Common mistakes

- **"Aucun tournoi" (no tournament).** Nothing has been created in this box yet → click **Créer un tournoi**.
- **An athlete cannot register.** The tournament started without **Inscriptions ouvertes pendant le tournoi**, the bracket is already drawn, the tournament is full or archived → tick the box in **Modifier**, raise **Max participants**, or unarchive the tournament.
- **The ranking looks frozen.** Some scores are still pending: only validated scores count → validate them in **Scores à valider**.
- **A past tournament clutters the list.** Close it (**Distribuer l'ELO et clôturer**) to move it to the **Historique**, or **Archiver** to take it out of the lists: its results, rankings and ELO are kept, and **Désarchiver** brings it back.

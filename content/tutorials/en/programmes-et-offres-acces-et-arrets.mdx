## Before you start

Athlete programs live in **Marketplace → Programmes athlètes**, your offers for other boxes in **Marketplace → Mes offres**. These pages are for the owner only: a coach has no access. On-screen labels are in French, so they are quoted as they appear.

## Give or remove access

1. Click **Accès** on the program card: the **Accès au programme** window opens.
2. Under **Donner l'accès à un membre**, pick a member in « Choisir un membre actif… ».
3. **Assigner (offert)** gives free access, recorded as such. If the program has a price, **Assigner** (payé au comptoir) records a counter payment: the amount is prefilled with the price, can only be lowered, and goes into the cash log, where it can no longer be corrected.
4. **Ont accès** (who has access) shows the origin: « Acheté (Stripe) », « Payé au comptoir », « Offert par la box » or « Inscription héritée (origine non vérifiée) ». <Screenshot src="/tutorials/programmes-et-offres-acces-et-arrets/1.png" alt="Accès au programme window: a member selected, the Assigner (offert) and Assigner payé au comptoir buttons, the Ont accès list with three origins" />
5. **Retirer** (remove): free access goes at once. Access paid at the counter asks for confirmation (**Retirer l’accès**): any refund is up to you. Stripe access shows « Accès payé », qui se retire par un remboursement Stripe (removed through a Stripe refund).

## Deactivate or delete a program or an offer

1. **Désactiver** takes a program off sale (**Inactif** badge), **Activer** puts it back.
2. **Supprimer** with no running Stripe buyer: every buyer loses access immediately, with no way to restore it.
3. If buyers still pay through Stripe: « … paie encore le programme par Stripe : tu ne peux pas le supprimer tel quel. » Pick **Désactiver le programme** (subscribers keep paying) or **Arrêter l’abonnement à la fin de sa période et désactiver le programme**, confirmed with **Arrêter et désactiver**: everyone keeps access until the end of their paid period, with no new charge. You can delete it after the last subscription ends. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/programmes-et-offres-acces-et-arrets/2.png" alt="“Supprimer le programme … ?” window with Stripe buyers: Désactiver le programme and stop-then-deactivate choices" />
4. In **Mes offres**, the bin of an offer paid by subscribed boxes likewise offers **Désactiver l’offre** (it goes back to **Brouillon**, draft) or **Arrêter et désactiver**. With no running Stripe subscription, deleting ends the subscribed boxes' subscription, but the sessions already placed with them stay. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/programmes-et-offres-acces-et-arrets/3.png" alt="“Supprimer l’offre … ?” window with subscribed boxes: Désactiver l’offre and stop-then-deactivate choices" />

## Manage sessions in bulk

1. **Séances** on the card opens the editor, week by week.
2. The moon icon **Repos** turns a day into a rest day: it no longer accepts sessions.
3. **Dupliquer sem. 1 → 2** copies the sessions and rest days of the displayed week to the next one, on top of what it already holds.
4. **Sélectionner** adds a checkbox to each session: tick them (or **Toute la semaine**), then **Supprimer la sélection**. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/programmes-et-offres-acces-et-arrets/4.png" alt="Session editor in selection mode: two sessions ticked, one rest day, the Supprimer la sélection and Dupliquer sem. 1 → 2 buttons" />
5. **Tout supprimer** deletes the sessions of the displayed week, without touching other weeks or the Whiteboard.
6. **Exporter** downloads the displayed week as CSV.

<Callout type="warning">
**A deleted session does not come back.** Export the week before a **Tout supprimer** if you want to keep it.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **A member is missing from the list.** Only active box members without current access appear.
- **« Montant encaissé supérieur au prix du programme (…) : une remise comptoir descend, elle ne monte pas. »** → enter an amount lower than or equal to the price.
- **« Montant encaissé invalide : indique une somme positive. »** → fix the amount.
- **« Suppression pas encore possible ».** All remaining subscriptions are already scheduled to stop → come back after the date shown.
- **« Stripe a refusé l’arrêt … »** Nothing is deactivated, the other stops are done → run the action again.
- **« Ce jour contient des séances : déplacez-les ou supprimez-les avant de le passer en repos. »** → empty the day first.
- **« Dupliquer » is greyed out.** The week has no session, or it is the last week of a fixed program → raise the **Durée (semaines)** with **Modifier**. **Exporter** is only greyed out on an empty week.

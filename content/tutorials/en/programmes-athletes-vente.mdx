## Before you start

**Marketplace → Programmes athlètes** (`/programming/athletes`) gathers the coaching programs you sell to your athletes. Gym access plans (subscription, drop-in, pack, trial) and promo codes now live in **Formules** (see « Formules d'accès à la salle ». To take payments online, payments must be enabled in **Réglages → Paiements**.

## Steps

1. Open **Marketplace → Programmes athlètes**. To sell online, first enable payments in **Réglages → Paiements**: the page does not remind you, and without it you can only grant access by hand. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/programmes-athletes-vente/1.png" alt="Programmes athlètes tab of Marketplace with a program card: price, buyers, Séances, Accès, Modifier buttons" />
2. Click **Créer un programme**: the **Nouveau programme** dialog asks for a **Titre**, a **Description** and a **Prix (€)**. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/programmes-athletes-vente/2.png" alt="Nouveau programme dialog: title, description, price, program type, duration" />
3. Choose the **Type de programme**: **Programme fixe** (« Durée définie (6, 8, 12 sem.) »), which opens **Durée (semaines)** and **Jours / semaine**, or **Ongoing** (« Programme continu »), with no end date and billed every month. Those fields sit in the same dialog as step 2.
4. **Actif (visible pour les athlètes)** is ticked by default: untick it if the program is not ready, then click **Créer le programme**; that box is what makes it appear on your public page.
5. To grant access without an online payment, click **Accès** on the card, pick an active member under **Donner l'accès à un membre**, then **Assigner (offert)** or **Assigner** (payé au comptoir) (amount prefilled with the price, can only be lowered). **Promo codes** are managed in **Formules**.

## Common mistakes

- **The offer does not appear on the athlete side.** It is not ticked as active and visible → tick the box and reload the public page.
- **Payment fails, or the button is missing.** Payments are not enabled for the box → finish the setup in **Réglages → Paiements**, then try again.
- **A promo code has no effect.** It has expired, or its discount duration is over → check the expiry date in **Formules → Codes promo**.
- **A member paid but has no content.** The program sold has no sessions yet → fill it in, or grant access to a complete program.

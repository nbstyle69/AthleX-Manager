## Before you start

**Marketplace → Programmes athlètes** (`/programming/athletes`) gathers the coaching programs you sell to your athletes. Gym access plans (subscription, drop-in, pack, trial) and promo codes now live in **Formules** — see « Formules d'accès à la salle ». To take payments online, payments must be enabled in **Réglages → Paiements**.

## Steps

1. Open **Marketplace → Programmes athlètes**. If the page invites you to enable payments in order to sell your programs, do that from **Réglages → Paiements**: without it you can only grant access by hand.
2. Create your offer and choose its nature: a fixed-duration coaching program, a monthly subscription, or a drop-in.
3. Fill in the price, the duration in weeks or the commitment depending on the case, the description, and the terms shown to the visitor before payment. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/programmes-athletes-vente/1.png" alt="Offer form: price, duration, terms" />
4. Tick **Actif (visible pour les athlètes)** when the offer is ready: that is what makes it appear on your public page.
5. Use **Donner l'accès à un membre** to grant a program without an online payment. **Promo codes** are managed in **Formules**.

## Common mistakes

- **The offer does not appear on the athlete side.** It is not ticked as active and visible → tick the box and reload the public page.
- **Payment fails, or the button is missing.** Payments are not enabled for the box → finish the setup in **Réglages → Paiements**, then try again.
- **A promo code has no effect.** It has expired, or its discount duration is over → check the expiry date in **Formules → Codes promo**.
- **A member paid but has no content.** The program sold has no sessions yet → fill it in, or grant access to a complete program.

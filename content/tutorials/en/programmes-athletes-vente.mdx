## Before you start

**Programmes athlètes** gathers what you offer your members: coaching programs, subscription plans, drop-ins. To take payments online, payments must be enabled for the box; the platform commission is displayed on the page.

## Steps

1. Open **Programmes athlètes**. If the page invites you to enable payments in order to sell your programs, do that first: without it you can only grant access by hand.
2. Create your offer and choose its nature: a fixed-duration coaching program, a monthly subscription, or a drop-in.
3. Fill in the price, the duration in weeks or the commitment depending on the case, the description, and the terms shown to the visitor before payment. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/programmes-athletes-vente/1.png" alt="Offer form: price, duration, terms" />
4. Tick **Actif (visible pour les athlètes)** when the offer is ready: that is what makes it appear on your public page.
5. Optionally add a promo code (discount, and discount duration for subscriptions), or use **Donner l'accès à un membre** to grant a program without an online payment.

## Common mistakes

- **The offer does not appear on the athlete side.** It is not ticked as active and visible → tick the box and reload the public page.
- **Payment fails, or the button is missing.** Payments are not enabled for the box → enable them from the page, then try again.
- **A promo code has no effect.** It has expired, or its discount duration is over → check the expiry date shown in the promo codes section.
- **A member paid but has no content.** The program sold has no sessions yet → fill it in, or grant access to a complete program.

## Before you start

A week template ("semaine type") is a frozen copy of a Whiteboard week, stored in your box so you can place it again whenever you want. It is internal: it is never sold and never exposed to anyone.

## Steps

1. On the Whiteboard, display the week you want to keep as a model.
2. Click **Enregistrer comme semaine type**, give it a meaningful name (for example "Hybrid week – volume"), then confirm. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/semaines-types/1.png" alt="Save week as template dialog" />
3. To place it again, go to the target week and open **Programmation**: the dialog lists **Mes semaines types** (your templates) and the **Programmations Marketplace** your box subscribes to.
4. Pick the template, the target week, then the audience under **Qui voit ces WOD ?** — without that choice the placement is refused.
5. Apply. WODs are copied onto the days of the target week; sessions that already carry a score or a completion are never overwritten.

## Common mistakes

- **"I saved a week template but my Marketplace offer is still empty."** "Enregistrer comme semaine type" does not populate an offer: **Copier vers une offre** (copy to an offer) is what copies a week into an offer week → use that button.
- **The Programmation dialog is empty.** No template exists and the box has no subscription → save a week, or subscribe to an offer in the Marketplace.
- **The placed WODs are visible to nobody.** The audience chosen at placement time was "nobody yet" → reopen the sessions and set their audience.
- **Did deleting a template erase my weeks?** No: weeks already placed on the Whiteboard stay, only the model disappears.

## Before you start

A week template ("semaine type") is a frozen copy of a Whiteboard week, stored in your box so you can place it again whenever you want. It is internal: it is never sold and never exposed to anyone.

## Steps

1. On the Whiteboard, display the week you want to keep as a model. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/semaines-types/3.png" alt="Whiteboard week to save as a template" />
2. Click **Enregistrer comme semaine type**, give it a meaningful **Nom** (for example "Hybrid week – volume") — or pick a template to overwrite —, then click **Enregistrer**. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/semaines-types/1.png" alt="Enregistrer comme semaine type dialog with a name entered" />
3. To place it again, go to the target week and open **Programmation**: the dialog lists **Mes semaines types** (your templates) and the **Programmations Marketplace** your box subscribes to. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/semaines-types/2.png" alt="Programmation dialog with a template selected and Toute la box chosen" />
4. Pick the template, the target week, then the audience under **Qui voit ces WOD ?** — without that choice the placement is refused.
5. Click **Appliquer** (or **Remplacer les WOD vierges** if the target week already has WODs). WODs are copied onto the days of the target week; sessions that already carry a score or a completion are never overwritten.

## Common mistakes

- **"I saved a week template but my Marketplace offer is still empty."** "Enregistrer comme semaine type" does not populate an offer: **Copier vers une offre** (copy to an offer) is what copies a week into an offer week → use that button (owner) from the Whiteboard, or the template's copy icon in **Programmation**.
- **The Programmation dialog is empty.** No template exists and the box has no subscription → save a week, or subscribe the box to an offer in the Marketplace (owner).
- **The placed WODs are visible to nobody.** The audience chosen at placement time was "nobody yet" → reopen the sessions and set their audience.
- **Did deleting a template erase my weeks?** No: weeks already placed on the Whiteboard stay, only the model disappears.

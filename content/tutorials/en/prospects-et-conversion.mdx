## Before you start

**Prospects** follows two populations that never mix: **visitors with no account** who came through the trial flow, and **members already signed up** who did a first session without a subscription. Three tabs: **Essais** (trials), **Adhérents** (members), **Créneaux RDV** (appointment slots). On-screen labels are in French, so they are quoted as they appear.

## Steps

1. Open the **Essais** tab. Five columns: **Essai réservé** (booked a seat), **Venu** (marked present), **Pas venu** (missed the class), **Relancé** (contacted after the trial), **Converti** (became a member). Prospects marked as lost are grouped at the bottom under **Perdus**. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/prospects-et-conversion/1.png" alt="Trials tab: five pipeline columns" />
2. Let attendance do the work: « Venu / Pas venu » fills itself when you take attendance for the class in **Horaires**. On the card you can also force **Venu** or **Pas venu** by hand.
3. After the trial, call or write to the prospect, then click **Marquer relancé**: the card moves to **Relancé** and you know where you stand the following week. Each card carries the email, the phone, the booked slot and the booking date.
4. Close the loop: **Converti** when they sign up, or the cross to mark them lost. A converted prospect is not a member yet: send them a personal invitation with their plan (see « Inviter un membre nominativement »), or let them subscribe from your public page.
5. **Adhérents** tab: this pipeline lists signed-up members without a subscription as soon as they are marked present at a first session. Five columns: **Essai réalisé** (waiting for feedback), **Feedback reçu** (gave their rating out of 5 and a comment), **RDV pris** (appointment booked), **Offre proposée** (offer proposed), **Converti**. Use **Proposer une offre** → **Choisir une offre…** → **Envoyer l'offre**, then **Marquer converti** once it is signed. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/prospects-et-conversion/2.png" alt="Members tab: feedback, offer proposal, conversion" />
6. **Créneaux RDV** tab: the **Ouvrir un créneau** block — date, start and end time, capacity, coach (optional), then **Ajouter le créneau**. Open slots are listed under **Créneaux à venir** with how full they are. They are for meeting a prospect for an appointment or a tour: they are independent from classes and are not used by the trial flow.

<Callout>
Two boards, two origins: the **Essais** tab comes from your public page and visitors with no account; the **Adhérents** tab comes from your attendance records. No prospect ever moves from one tab to the other.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **A card is stuck in Essai réservé.** Attendance for the class was never taken → take it in **Horaires**, or force **Venu** / **Pas venu** on the card.
- **You look for a trial prospect in Membres.** They have no AthleX account: they live in **Prospects** until they sign up.
- **« Proposer une offre » shows no offer.** You have no plan yet → create one in **Formules**.
- **A converted prospect does not appear in Abonnés.** Conversion records your decision, it does not create a paid subscription → go through a personal invitation or an online subscription.

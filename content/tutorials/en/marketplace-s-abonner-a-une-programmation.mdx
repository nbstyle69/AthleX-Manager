## Before you start

The **Marketplace** is the box-to-box channel: you buy or sell programmings there. What you receive lands on your Whiteboard.

## Steps

1. Open the **Marketplace**, tab **Catalogue**, and filter by discipline or level to browse the offers published by other boxes. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/marketplace-s-abonner-a-une-programmation/1.png" alt="Marketplace catalogue with filters" />
2. Open an offer: you see its goal, its target audience, the number of WODs per week, the billing (monthly, one-off or free) and a preview of week 1.
3. Click **S'abonner** (subscribe) and confirm. Your own offers are never offered for subscription.
4. Decide how weeks are placed. Nothing is placed on its own until **Application automatique chaque semaine** is ticked; once ticked, the due week is placed by itself on Sunday at 18:00, week 1 on the first Sunday.
5. If you would rather keep control, leave the box unticked and place weeks whenever you want from the Whiteboard, using the **Programmation** button.

## Common mistakes

- **Nothing arrives on the Whiteboard after subscribing.** Automatic placement is unticked — that is the default → place the week from the Whiteboard, or tick the box.
- **Received sessions are seen by no member.** Automatic placements arrive as "Personne encore" (nobody yet) → open them and set their audience.
- **A received session cannot be edited.** The content of a Marketplace programming is not editable; you only choose who sees it, and you may move or delete it.
- **"déjà abonnée" (already subscribed).** Another of your boxes already subscribes to this offer → check the active box in the sidebar selector.

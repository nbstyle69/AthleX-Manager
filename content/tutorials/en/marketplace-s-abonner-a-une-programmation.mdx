## Before you start

The **Marketplace** has three tabs; **Catalogue** and **Mes offres** form the box-to-box channel: you buy or sell programmings there. What you receive lands on your Whiteboard. The page is reserved for the owner: a coach has no access to it.

## Steps

1. Open the **Marketplace**, tab **Catalogue**, and filter by discipline, level or **Gratuit** (free) to browse the offers published by other boxes. Each card shows the publishing box, the goal, the number of WODs and the duration. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/marketplace-s-abonner-a-une-programmation/1.png" alt="Marketplace catalogue with filters and one offer" />
2. Click **Voir le détail** (see details): you get the goal, the target audience, the equipment, the WODs per week, the price (**Gratuit**, « €/mois » for a monthly subscription, or a one-off amount) and the day-by-day preview of week 1. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/marketplace-s-abonner-a-une-programmation/2.png" alt="Marketplace offer details with the week 1 preview" />
3. Click **S'abonner** (subscribe): the window lists your boxes, all ticked (untick those to exclude), then click **Confirmer l'abonnement**. A paid offer goes through Stripe payment, one box at a time. The card then shows **Abonné** (subscribed) and the subscription settings. Your own offers are never offered for subscription.
4. Pick the **Couleur** (colour): it is what identifies the sessions of this programming on the Whiteboard. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/marketplace-s-abonner-a-une-programmation/3.png" alt="Subscribed offer with colour, automatic placement and unsubscribe link" />
5. Decide how weeks are placed. Nothing is placed on its own until **Application automatique chaque semaine** is ticked; once ticked, the due week is placed by itself on Sunday at 18:00, week 1 on the first Sunday. Unticked, you place weeks whenever you want from the Whiteboard, using the **Programmation** button.
6. To stop, use **Se désabonner** (unsubscribe) on the same card: a confirmation offers to also remove the sessions already placed from next week on. The current week and the past are never deleted, so scores and ELO are kept. The box is ticked by default. For a paid offer, the Stripe subscription stops at the end of the current period, whose date the card shows.

## Common mistakes

- **Nothing arrives on the Whiteboard after subscribing.** Automatic placement is unticked (that is the default) → place the week from the Whiteboard, or tick the box.
- **Received sessions are seen by no member.** Automatic placements arrive as "Personne encore" (nobody yet) → open them and set their audience.
- **A received session cannot be edited.** The content of a Marketplace programming is not editable; you only choose who sees it, and you may move or delete it.
- **"déjà abonnée" (already subscribed) in the S'abonner window.** That box already receives the offer: it stays greyed out and is not subscribed twice → tick only the other boxes.

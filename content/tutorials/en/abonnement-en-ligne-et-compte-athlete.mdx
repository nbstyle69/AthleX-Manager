## Before you start

This journey needs nothing from you: the athlete pays on your public page, Stripe collects, and they show up in **Abonnés**. It assumes **Stripe Connect** is connected in **Réglages → Paiements**, and that the plan is **Active** with a price above €0. On-screen labels are in French, so they are quoted as they appear.

## Steps

1. On `athlexapp.eu/box/<your-slug>`, section **Abonnements**, the athlete clicks **S'abonner** on the plan they want (the button carries the price). For a drop-in or a pack, in the **À la carte** section, the button reads **Acheter**. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/abonnement-en-ligne-et-compte-athlete/1.png" alt="Subscriptions section of the public page" />
2. They read the **Récapitulatif du contrat** (contract summary): price including tax, commitment, sessions per week or unlimited access, description, and the **conditions / mentions** you typed on the plan. The link to your **Conditions générales (PDF)** appears there if it is uploaded in Réglages. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/abonnement-en-ligne-et-compte-athlete/2.png" alt="Contract summary before payment" />
3. **Payer par carte** takes them to **Stripe Checkout**, where they enter their email and payment method: card everywhere, plus **SEPA** direct debit when the plan is priced in euros. The window warns them first: « Utilise l'e-mail de ton compte AthleX au paiement » — that address decides which account is credited, and if they have none yet, the subscription is attached when they sign up.
4. After payment their access is attached to your box automatically, with no approval from you. You see them in **Abonnés**, filter **Salle**, with the amount actually charged and their next due date.
5. They manage the rest from `athlexapp.eu/compte` — **Mon compte**, « Gère tes informations, ton abonnement et tes crédits de séances »: their plan, its status, the monthly amount and the **Prochaine échéance**, their **Drop-in & Carnets** with remaining credits and validity dates, and the programs they bought. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/abonnement-en-ligne-et-compte-athlete/3.png" alt="The athlete's account area" />
6. **Changer de formule** (change plan) applies an **immediate proration**: they pick the new plan, **Confirmer**, and Stripe adjusts the invoice. **Résilier** keeps access « jusqu'à la fin de la période déjà payée », with no further charge.
7. If their plan is still under **engagement**, the button becomes **Demander une résiliation**: a reason (**Déménagement** — moving, **Santé / blessure grave** — health, **Autre motif**), a message and a **justificatif (PDF ou image, max 10 Mo)** (supporting document). The request lands in **Abonnés**, where you accept or refuse it.

<Callout>
Pausing a subscription is decided **on the box side**, from **Abonnés** (**Geler** / **Reprendre**). The athlete sees « Abonnement en pause — reprise le … Contacte ta box pour le réactiver »; they cannot trigger it themselves.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **The S'abonner button is missing.** Stripe Connect is not connected, or the plan is inactive / priced at €0 → see **Réglages → Paiements** and **Formules**.
- **The athlete paid with a different email address.** Their access went to another AthleX account: the payment email is what counts.
- **They cannot see their subscription in « Mon compte ».** They are signed in with another account, or the bank has not confirmed the payment yet.
- **They want to cancel but only see « Demander une résiliation ».** Their commitment is still running: early cancellation goes through your decision, with a supporting document.
- **They say they were charged twice after changing plans.** Immediate proration generates an adjustment: the detail is in their billing history, visible in **Abonnés**.

## Before you start

This journey needs nothing from you: the athlete pays on your public page, Stripe collects, and they show up in **Abonnés**. It assumes **Stripe Connect** is connected in **Réglages → Paiements**, and that the plan is **Active** with a price above €0. On-screen labels are in French, so they are quoted as they appear.

## Steps

1. On `athlexapp.eu/box/<your-slug>`, section **Abonnements**, the athlete clicks **S'abonner** on the plan they want (the button carries the price). For a drop-in or a pack, in the **À la carte** section, the button reads **Acheter**. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/abonnement-en-ligne-et-compte-athlete/1.png" alt="Subscriptions and À la carte sections of the public page: S'abonner and Acheter buttons" />
2. They read the **Récapitulatif du contrat** (contract summary): price including tax, commitment, sessions per week or unlimited access, description, and the **conditions / mentions** you typed on the plan. The link to your **Conditions générales (PDF)** appears there if it is uploaded in Réglages. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/abonnement-en-ligne-et-compte-athlete/2.png" alt="Contract summary before payment" />
3. **Payer par carte** takes them to **Stripe Checkout**, where they enter their email (imposed if they are already signed in to their AthleX account) and payment method: card everywhere, plus **SEPA** direct debit for a subscription in euros. The window warns them first: « Utilise l'e-mail de ton compte AthleX au paiement ». That address decides which account is credited, and if they have none yet, the subscription is attached when they sign up.
4. After payment their access is attached to your box automatically, with no approval from you. Before paying, they choose their **Jour de prélèvement** (billing day), from the 1st to the 10th of the month. The summary shows what is charged today (the proration until the next charge), then the monthly amount and its date; if the next chosen day is very close, « Aucun prélèvement aujourd'hui » (no charge today) and « Premier prélèvement le … » (first charge on …) replace the proration. After that, they are charged every month on that day. You see them in **Abonnés**, filter **Salle**, with the amount actually charged and their next due date. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/abonnement-en-ligne-et-compte-athlete/5.png" alt="Billing day choice, from the 1st to the 10th, with today’s amount and the monthly amount" />
5. They manage the rest from `athlexapp.eu/compte`, on **Mon compte**, « Gère tes informations, ton abonnement et tes crédits de séances »: their **Informations personnelles**, their plan, its status, the monthly amount and the **Prochaine échéance**, their **Drop-in & Carnets** with remaining credits and validity dates, and **Mes programmes**. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/abonnement-en-ligne-et-compte-athlete/4.png" alt="The athlete’s “My account” area" />
6. Under their subscription, **Changer de formule** (change plan) shows « Nouvelle formule (prorata immédiat) »: they tick the new plan then **Confirmer**, and Stripe adjusts the invoice straight away. **Résilier** then **Confirmer la résiliation** keeps access « jusqu'à la fin de la période déjà payée », with no further charge; the screen then shows « Résiliation programmée le … ». <Screenshot src="/tutorials/abonnement-en-ligne-et-compte-athlete/3.png" alt="My account: « Nouvelle formule (prorata immédiat) » choice with the Confirmer and Annuler buttons" />
7. If their plan is still under **engagement**, the button becomes **Demander une résiliation**: a reason (**Déménagement** for moving, **Santé / blessure grave** for health, **Autre motif**), a message and a **justificatif (PDF ou image, max 10 Mo)** (supporting document), then **Envoyer la demande**. The request lands in **Abonnés**, where you accept or refuse it.

<Callout>
Pausing a subscription is decided **on the box side**, from **Abonnés** (**Geler** / **Reprendre**). The athlete sees « Abonnement en pause », avec la date de reprise et « Contacte ta box pour le réactiver »; they cannot trigger it themselves.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **The plan does not appear on the page.** It is inactive or priced at €0 → see **Formules**. **« Cette box n'a pas encore activé les paiements. »** at payment time: Stripe Connect is not connected → see **Réglages → Paiements**.
- **The athlete paid with a different email address.** If they were not signed in, their access went to the address typed at Stripe: the payment email is what counts.
- **They cannot see their subscription in « Mon compte ».** They are signed in with another account, or the bank has not confirmed the payment yet.
- **They want to cancel but only see « Demander une résiliation ».** Their commitment is still running: early cancellation goes through your decision, with a supporting document.
- **They say they were charged twice after changing plans.** Immediate proration generates an adjustment: the detail is in their billing history, visible in **Abonnés**.

## Before you start

Your gym pays AthleX to use the Manager (**Plan Complet**). A banner at the top of every page tells you where that subscription stands: only the owner and co-owner see it, never the coach. Archiving the gym is decided by the AthleX team: once it is scheduled, a second banner shows for you and your staff, coaches included. On-screen labels are in French, so they are quoted as they appear.

## Steps: your gym's AthleX subscription

1. **During the free trial**, the banner shows « Essai gratuit · J-… », the days left and « Expire le … » (ends on). It turns orange at 7 days, then red at 3 days with « Plus que quelques jours ! Souscris pour ne rien perdre. ». Click it (**Voir →**) to open the pricing page and pick **Mensuel** (monthly) or **Annuel** (yearly).
2. **Active subscription**: green banner « Plan Complet actif » and a **Gérer →** (manage) button. It opens the billing portal (« Redirection vers le portail de facturation… »), where you change your card and find your invoices.
3. **Payment declined**: red banner « Paiement en échec » qui invite à mettre à jour la carte. The Manager stays open « le temps de régulariser » (while you sort it out). Click **Mettre à jour la carte** to reach the portal and change your payment method. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/abonnement-athlex-et-archivage/1.png" alt="Red banner “Paiement en échec” asking to update the card at the top of the Dashboard, with the Mettre à jour la carte button" />
4. **Trial over or subscription stopped**: a « Ton essai gratuit est terminé » window covers the Manager. « Tes données sont conservées. » (your data is kept). Click **Souscrire maintenant**; if you have just paid, click **J'ai déjà payé** (actualiser).

## Steps: scheduled archiving of the gym

1. **Who decides.** Archiving is started by the AthleX team, not from your Manager, and no button lets you cancel it: to talk about it, write to **Support**.
2. **The banner.** On every page: « Archivage programmé : … sera archivée au plus tard le …, à la fin du dernier abonnement. Les nouvelles adhésions, invitations et ventes sont fermées. » (archived at the latest on that date; new memberships, invitations and sales are closed). <Screenshot src="/tutorials/abonnement-athlex-et-archivage/2.png" alt="Dashboard with the orange banner “Archivage programmé : … sera archivée au plus tard le …”" />
3. **What carries on.** Every paying subscription (members through Stripe, programs, Marketplace offers sold or bought, your gym's AthleX subscription) stops at the end of its paid period, or at once if it is unpaid. Everyone keeps access until then. When nothing pays any more, the gym is archived automatically; the banner date therefore follows the remaining subscriptions.
4. **What is closed in the Manager.** Changing the invite code, sending an invitation, **Encaissement reçu** in **Abonnés** (« … les ventes au comptoir sont fermées. »), **Reprendre** on a frozen subscription (**Geler** is still possible) and changing your gym's AthleX subscription. Those refusals open in a window titled « Archivage programmé », except the counter one, which shows in a red frame on the **Abonnés** page. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/abonnement-athlex-et-archivage/3.png" alt="“Archivage programmé” window: “Cette box n'accepte plus de nouveaux membres.”, after Générer un nouveau code" />
5. **What a visitor sees.** Your public page only shows the gym's name, « Cette box n'accepte plus de nouveaux membres. » and **Voir les boxs →**: no more trial session, subscription or purchase, and the gym leaves the directory. An invitation link opens « Invitation indisponible » with the same message and « Demande un nouveau lien à ta box, ou trouve-la dans l'annuaire. ». <Screenshot src="/tutorials/abonnement-athlex-et-archivage/4.png" alt="Public page of a gym being archived: gym name, “Cette box n'accepte plus de nouveaux membres.” and Voir les boxs →" />
6. **Once archived**, the gym disappears from the Manager. If it was your only gym, signing in takes you to your athlete space.

<Callout type="warning">
An archived gym does not come back: talk to AthleX support before the date.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **« Aucun abonnement actif détecté. Si tu viens de payer, patiente quelques secondes puis réessaie. »** The payment is not confirmed yet → wait a moment, then **J'ai déjà payé** (actualiser).
- **« L'abonnement de cette box s'arrête le … : il ne peut plus être modifié. »** Your gym is scheduled for archiving: its AthleX subscription stops on that date.
- **« Portail indisponible » or « Erreur réseau » under the banner.** The portal could not open → try again, then write to **Support**.
- **You cannot find the subscription in Réglages.** It is not there: everything goes through the banner and **Gérer →**.

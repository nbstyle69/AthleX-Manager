## Before you start

Every WOD carries an audience, set in the **Qui reçoit ce WOD ?** block of the editor. It decides which box members see the session, provided the WOD is published.

## Steps

1. On the Whiteboard, open the WOD with its pencil (**Modifier**): **Qui reçoit ce WOD ?** is at the very top of the dialog. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/visibilite-d-un-wod/1.png" alt="Audience block with its three options" />
2. Pick **Toute la box** (whole box) for shared programming: every active member sees the session as soon as it is published, or at the time chosen with **Programmer**. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/visibilite-d-un-wod/4.png" alt="Toute la box audience selected" />
3. Pick **Ces groupes** (these groups) and tick the groups for a session reserved to one level, time slot or program. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/visibilite-d-un-wod/2.png" alt="Audience selector with Ces groupes (these groups) selected and one group ticked" />
4. Pick **Personne encore** (nobody yet) to keep the session in reserve: no box member sees it in the app (except members of a program ticked under **Mes programmes athlètes**), and it stays visible in the back-office to the owner and the coaches. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/visibilite-d-un-wod/3.png" alt="Personne encore audience selected" />
5. Click **Enregistrer** (or **Créer le WOD**). The Whiteboard card shows the chosen audience ("Visible par toute la box", "Visible par personne" or the group names): check it at a glance before the class starts. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/visibilite-d-un-wod/5.png" alt="Whiteboard cards with the Visible par toute la box, group and Visible par personne badges" />

## Common mistakes

- **"My athletes see nothing although the WOD is there."** The WOD is set to "Personne encore" ("Visible par personne" badge), or **Publier** is off (**Brouillon** badge) → change the audience or turn **Publier** on.
- **Weeks received from the Marketplace do not appear on the athlete side.** As long as you have not placed a week by hand, automatic placements arrive as "Personne encore" → open **Programmation** and place the week choosing who sees it (**Remplacer les WOD vierges** if it is already placed): that choice becomes the one for the next automatic placements.
- **A member of the group still does not see the session.** They are not in the ticked group, or are no longer an active member → check their member record in Membres (owner).
- **The session shows up late.** The WOD is set to **Programmer**: the chosen **Heure** is the publication time → move it earlier, or pick **Maintenant**.

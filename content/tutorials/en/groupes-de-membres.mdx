## Before you start

A group gathers members (a level, a time slot, a specific follow-up). It is used as a WOD audience and as a filter in the member list. Groups are managed under **Groupes**.

## Steps

1. Open **Groupes** and create a new group: give it a name a busy coach will understand ("Competitors", "Lunch", "Beginners"). <Screenshot src="/tutorials/groupes-de-membres/1.png" alt="Box group list" />
2. Save, then open the group to **Ajouter un membre** (add a member); the search covers the active members of the box.
3. Go to **Membres** to check the result: the group filter lets you display one group at a time.
4. Then use the group as an audience: in a WOD, choose **Ces groupes** and tick it.

## Common mistakes

- **"Aucun groupe. Crée des groupes de cours d'abord."** The audience selector stays empty until a group exists → create one under Groupes.
- **A member does not receive the group's WODs.** They were never added to the group, or their account is no longer active → check their member record.
- **A deleted group leaves WODs with no audience.** Sessions targeted at that group reach nobody → reopen them and pick another audience.

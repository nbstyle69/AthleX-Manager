## Before you start

A group gathers members (a level, a time slot, a specific follow-up). It is used as a WOD audience and as a filter in the member list. Groups are managed under **Groupes**.

## Steps

1. Open **Groupes** and create a new group: give it a name a busy coach will understand ("Competitors", "Lunch", "Beginners"). <Screenshot src="/tutorials/groupes-de-membres/1.png" alt="Box group list" />
2. Click **Créer et ajouter des membres** (create and add members): the group detail opens. In **Ajouter des membres**, click **Ajouter** next to each member; the search (by username) and the **Filtres** cover the active members of the box. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/groupes-de-membres/3.png" alt="Add members block of a group detail" />
3. Go to **Membres** to check the result: click **Filtres**, then pick the group in the **Tous les groupes** list to display only that group. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/groupes-de-membres/2.png" alt="Members page and its group filter" />
4. Then use the group as an audience: in a WOD, choose **Ces groupes** and tick it.

## Common mistakes

- **"Ces groupes" is greyed out in a WOD** (tooltip "Aucun groupe dans cette box"). No group exists yet → create one under **Groupes**.
- **A member does not receive the group's WODs.** They were never added to the group, or their account is no longer active → in **Membres**, check their **Groupes** and **Statut** columns.
- **A deleted group makes its WODs visible to the whole box.** The confirmation says so: « Les WOD réservés à ce seul groupe deviendront visibles par tous les membres de la box. » → before deleting, reopen those WODs and pick another audience.

## Before you start

Nothing is visible by default: every WOD, every class and every news item reaches the app because you decided so. This tutorial lists what your athlete sees, and the Manager setting that drives it. On-screen labels are in French, so they are quoted as they appear.

## Steps

1. **WODs.** The **Qui reçoit ce WOD ?** block of the modal decides: **Toute la box** (every athlete), **Ces groupes** (only the ticked groups), **Personne encore** (visible to you alone, to prepare ahead). As long as a WOD sits on **Personne encore**, nobody sees it in the app. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/ce-que-voient-les-membres-dans-l-app/1.png" alt="The « Qui reçoit ce WOD ? » block" />
2. **Groups.** A group has an opening mode shown on its card in **Groupes**: **Semaine** (the member sees the whole week at once) or **Jour par jour** (they discover the session on the day). Same WOD, two different experiences.
3. **Athlete programs.** A program session has no date: « chaque acheteur la reçoit la semaine N, jour J après son démarrage » (each buyer gets it on week N, day D after their own start), and it is « visible par les acheteurs de ce programme uniquement ». Ticking a program in **Mes programmes athlètes** adds the session to its active members, even if the WOD is set to **Personne encore** for the box.
4. **The schedule.** The athlete sees the classes open for booking, with the seats left, and books or cancels from the app. Whatever is not published from **Horaires** does not exist for them.
5. **News and messages.** News items appear in their feed; messages target the box or a group. See « Actualités et messages ».
6. **Their subscription.** Plan, status, next due date, drop-in and pack credits, programs bought: everything is in their **Mon compte** area, fed by what you see in **Abonnés**.

<Callout>
A WOD on **Personne encore** stays visible to the members of a ticked program: the « Mes programmes athlètes » line adds to box visibility, it does not replace it.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **"My members cannot see the WOD."** It stayed on **Personne encore**, or it is limited to a group they do not belong to.
- **One member sees the week, another only the day.** That is the opening mode of their respective groups, not a bug.
- **An athlete cannot find their program session on the right day.** Program weeks are relative to **their own** start date: two buyers are not on the same week at the same time.
- **A new member sees nothing.** They are in no group and your WODs are restricted to groups → switch them to **Toute la box** or add the member to the right group.
- **They cannot see their subscription.** They use an account other than the email of their payment or their invitation.

## Before you start

Members arrive through four routes (public trial, personal invitation, online subscription, box code), compared in « Les quatre façons d'entrer dans ta box ». The **Membres** page then lists everybody, with search, filters and access to contracts.

## Steps

1. Open **Membres**: the counter at the top shows how many records are displayed out of the total, and the search accepts a name or an email. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/membres-et-formules/1.png" alt="Member list with search and filters" />
2. Narrow it down with **Filtres**: by level, by group (the **Tous les groupes** list), or sort by **ELO**. Clicking the **Contrat** column header sorts members by plan, which groups together those on **Illimité** (unlimited). <Screenshot src="/tutorials/membres-et-formules/4.png" alt="Filters panel of the Membres page" />
3. The **Contrat** column shows each member's plan (or **Illimité**); click it to assign another one. The **Contrats** button opens the **Contrats / Abonnements** panel: each plan with its price, its quota, its number of members and its **Cours inclus** (the groups the plan puts its members in). Due dates are read in **Abonnés**. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/membres-et-formules/3.png" alt="Contrats / Abonnements panel open above the member list" />
4. Click **Fiche** on a row: the athlete's level, ELO, gender, **Records 1RM** and **Séries réalisées**. The account state is read in the **Statut** column: **Actif** or **Banni** (banned).
5. The plans themselves (price, duration, commitment) are created in **Formules** (plans, `/plans`): the member list consumes them, it does not define them. See « Formules d'accès à la salle ». <Screenshot src="/tutorials/membres-et-formules/2.png" alt="Plans page" />
6. To sign somebody up with their plan already attached, use **Invitations**: the personal link carries the plan and the payment mode. See « Inviter un membre nominativement ».

## Common mistakes

- **A member is missing from the list.** They have not finished their entry journey: an invitation stays in **Invitations** until it is accepted, and a trial prospect stays in **Prospects** until they have an account.
- **A member says they paid but has no contract.** The payment happened outside AthleX, or the checkout never completed → check the **Contrat** column, then **Abonnés** for online payments.
- **You cannot create a plan from the member list.** That is expected: creating and editing plans lives in **Formules** → open that page.
- **A coach cannot see the member list.** It is reserved to the owner and the co-owner (the coach gets « Accès refusé ») → sign in with an owner account.

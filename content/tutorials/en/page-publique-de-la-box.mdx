## Before you start

Your box has a public page, no account required: `athlexapp.eu/box/<your-slug>`. That is where visitors discover the gym, book a trial, subscribe, or buy a drop-in or a pack. Everything that feeds it is set in **Réglages** (settings), except the offers themselves, which live in **Formules**. On-screen labels are in French, so they are quoted as they appear.

## Steps

1. Open **Réglages**, section **Page publique**: that is where you set the address. Click **Modifier**, type your slug (lowercase, digits and hyphens), then **Enregistrer**. **Voir ma page** opens the page as a visitor sees it, **Copier le lien** puts it in your clipboard — that is the link for your Instagram bio and your Google Business profile. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/page-publique-de-la-box/1.png" alt="Public page section in Réglages: slug, view my page, copy link" />
2. Take care of the header: **Logo de la box** and **Bannière de la box**, in the first two sections of Réglages (PNG, JPG or WEBP). The logo also appears in the members' app; the banner is the wide image at the top of the page.
3. Fill in **Informations de la box**: box name, description, address, website, contact email, phone, Google Maps link and opening date. The description becomes the **À propos** section, the address and contacts feed the **Contact** block and the map, and the opening date is shown as a badge.
4. Upload your **Conditions générales (PDF)** (terms, 10 MB max). The document is offered to the future member **before payment**, from the contract summary, and stays available in their athlete area.
5. Check the page: depending on your active offers it shows **Séance d'essai**, **Abonnements**, **À la carte** (drop-ins and packs), **Programmations** (what you sell to other boxes) and **Contact**. A section with no content does not appear at all. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/page-publique-de-la-box/2.png" alt="Public box page: trial, subscriptions, à la carte" />

<Callout type="warning">
**A plan priced at €0 never appears on the public page — except the trial.** The page only shows subscriptions, drop-ins and packs priced above €0, plus the **Essai** (trial) offer, which is free by design. A paid plan set to €0 "to hide it" does disappear, but a €0 plan you would like to offer publicly will not show either: use the trial offer.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **A plan you created does not appear on the page.** It is not **Active**, or its price is €0 → activate it in **Formules** and give it a price.
- **The link you shared stopped working.** You changed the slug: the old address does not redirect → restore the old slug, or update your links.
- **The opening-hours section is empty on the public page.** Gym opening hours cannot be entered from the Manager yet (see below): what you fill in **Horaires** is the class schedule, not the opening hours shown in the window.
- **The terms PDF is not offered.** It only appears in the summary of a monthly subscription → check that it is uploaded in Réglages.

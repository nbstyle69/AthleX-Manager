## Before you start

Once the box subscribes, the weeks of a programming are placed on the Whiteboard: automatically if you asked for it, otherwise by hand from the **Programmation** button.

## Steps

1. On the Whiteboard, click **Programmation**. The dialog lists **Mes semaines types** (your week templates) then **Programmations Marketplace** with your active subscriptions (offer, publishing box, number of WODs and weeks). <Screenshot src="/tutorials/marketplace-appliquer-au-whiteboard/1.png" alt="Programmation dialog with a Marketplace subscription selected" />
2. Select the subscription to place: it gets highlighted and the placement fields appear.
3. Pick the **Semaine source** (the programming week, with its number of WODs) and the **Semaine cible** (the Monday the sessions start from). The dialog restates the exact date used.
4. Pick the audience under **Qui voit ces WOD ?**: **Toute la box** (whole box), **Ces groupes** (these groups) or **Personne encore** (nobody yet). That choice also becomes the default visibility of this subscription's automatic placement.
5. Click **Appliquer**. Sessions land on the days of the target week, in the subscription colour; those that already carry a score or a completion are not replaced.
6. Then adjust card by card: the audience remains yours, and a received session can be moved or deleted individually.

## Common mistakes

- **Automatic weeks do not arrive when expected.** With automatic placement, the due week is placed on Sunday at 18:00 for the week that follows; placing a week by hand realigns the anchor on what you have just done. {/* TODO Nab: vérifier libellé — la spec parle du lundi suivant l'abonnement, l'écran Marketplace annonce le dimanche 18h */}
- **A received session refuses to be edited.** Its content belongs to the programming and is not editable. You can delete it, card by card, or change its audience.
- **Nobody sees the placed sessions.** The audience stayed on "nobody yet", which is the default for automatic placements → set the audience.
- **The dialog offers no programming.** `Programmations Marketplace` shows `Aucun abonnement actif` (no active subscription) → go through the Marketplace.
- **Placing a week template is not a Marketplace offer.** The top of the dialog (`Mes semaines types`) places your own templates; it creates neither a subscription nor a published offer.

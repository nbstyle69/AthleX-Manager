## Before you start

Once the box subscribes, the weeks of a programming are placed on the Whiteboard: automatically if you asked for it, otherwise by hand from the **Programmation** button.

## Steps

1. On the Whiteboard, display the target week and click **Programmation**. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/marketplace-appliquer-au-whiteboard/1.png" alt="Programmation dialog with the Marketplace programmings" />
2. Under **Programmations Marketplace**, pick the offer, then the source week you want to place and the target week.
3. Pick the audience under **Qui voit ces WOD ?**: the whole box, some groups, or nobody for now. Without that choice the placement is refused.
4. Apply. Sessions land on the days of the target week; those that already carry a score or a completion are not replaced.
5. Then adjust card by card: the audience remains yours, and a received session can be moved or deleted individually.

## Common mistakes

- **Automatic weeks do not arrive when expected.** With automatic placement, the due week is placed on Sunday at 18:00 for the week that follows; placing a week by hand realigns the anchor on what you have just done. {/* TODO Nab: vérifier libellé — la spec parle du lundi suivant l'abonnement, l'écran Marketplace annonce le dimanche 18h */}
- **A received session refuses to be edited.** Its content belongs to the programming and is not editable. You can delete it, card by card, or change its audience.
- **Nobody sees the placed sessions.** The audience stayed on "nobody yet", which is the default for automatic placements → set the audience.
- **The dialog offers no programming.** The box has no active subscription → go through the Marketplace.

## Before you start

The sidebar, on the left (« Ouvrir le menu » button on a phone), leads to all your screens. **Messages** (Animation → Messages) gathers your groups' conversations. Coaches and owners both have access, with the differences pointed out below.

## The sidebar

1. Screens are sorted in groups: **Entraînement** (training), **Communauté** (community), **Animation** (events), **Pilotage** (steering). Click a group title to fold or unfold it; your choice is kept in this browser. A folded group still shows its counters, unread messages for example.
2. A coach only sees **Entraînement** (Whiteboard, Horaires, Créneaux types), **Animation** (Messages) and **Aide** (help). The Dashboard, **Support**, **Réglages** (settings) and the AthleX subscription badge are reserved to the owner. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/barre-laterale-et-messages/1.png" alt="Coach sidebar: Entraînement, Animation, Aide, then Mode clair, Mon espace athlète and Déconnexion" />
3. You run or coach several boxes? A selector shows up under the box name. Pick another box: the page reloads on it. It exists from two boxes on, coaches included. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/barre-laterale-et-messages/2.png" alt="Box selector open with two boxes, the active one ticked" />
4. At the bottom: **Mode clair** or **Mode sombre** switches the theme (kept in this browser), **Mon espace athlète** opens your athlete account, **Déconnexion** takes you back to the sign-in screen.

## Talk in a group

1. Open **Messages**: the **Conversations** column lists the box's groups. A coach only sees the groups he is a member of.
2. Click a group: the thread shows day by day (« Aujourd'hui », « Hier », then the date), your messages on the right, the others' on the left with their username and time. New messages arrive live. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/barre-laterale-et-messages/3.png" alt="Messages: Conversations column and thread of the Compétiteurs group over two days" />
3. Type in the « Message dans … » field at the bottom, then press Enter or click the send button.

## Send an announcement

1. At the bottom of the **Conversations** column, click **Envoyer une annonce**. From a group's page, the **Message** button opens the same page with that group already selected.
2. Fill in the « Envoyer un message » page: **Type** (**Annonce**, **Rappel**, **Alerte**, **Info**), **Titre (optionnel)** (title), **Message \*** and **Groupe cible** (target group: **Tous les membres** or one group). <Screenshot src="/tutorials/barre-laterale-et-messages/4.png" alt="Envoyer un message page with the Rappel type and the Compétiteurs target group" />
3. Click **Envoyer** (greyed out while the message is empty): you go back to **Messages**. **Annuler** gives up.

<Callout type="warning">
For now, only the owner who owns the box can send an announcement. For a coach or a co-owner, the database refuses it and a red banner shows up: use the group's thread instead.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **A coach does not see a group.** He is not part of it → the owner adds him to the group in **Groupes**.
- **« Aucun groupe » (no group).** The box has no group yet → the owner clicks **Créer un groupe**; for a coach, this link leads to « Accès refusé ».
- **Your message stays in the field.** Sending failed without an error message → reload the page and try again.
- **Red banner on « Envoyer un message ».** The database refused the announcement (coach or co-owner account, for example) → the banner text says why.
- **« Message envoyé au back office mais erreur app: … ».** The announcement is saved but not pushed to the app → do not send it again as is, reach your members another way.
- **The box selector does not show.** You are owner, co-owner or coach of a single box only.

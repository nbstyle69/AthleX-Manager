## Before you start

**Horaires & Créneaux** holds the bookable classes of the box, day by day. Recurring weekly grids live next door, in the schedule templates page.

## Steps

1. Open **Horaires & Créneaux** and move to the day you need.
2. Add a class and fill it in: **Type de cours** (class type) or a custom name, start time, duration, **Capacité** and an optional description. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/creneaux-et-reservations/1.png" alt="Class creation form" />
3. Assign a coach (optional): they appear on the athlete side and it helps your coaches find their classes.
4. Save: the class becomes bookable by members, up to its capacity.
5. On the day, open the class and click **Faire l'appel** to see who booked and mark attendance. You can also add a member by hand if somebody shows up without a booking.

## Common mistakes

- **"Aucun coach assigné à cette box." (no coach in this box).** No coach is registered in the settings → add one, then come back and assign the class.
- **A member cannot book.** Capacity is full, or the class is in the past → raise the capacity or open a second class.
- **"Aucun inscrit pour ce créneau" although the class is full.** You are looking at another day or another class of the same type → check the date shown at the top.
- **Next week's classes are missing.** They are generated from the schedule templates → open that page to lay out the recurring grid.

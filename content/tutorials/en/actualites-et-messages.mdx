## Before you start

Two tools, two uses: **Actualités** (news) publishes an article that stays in your box feed (title, text, image, likes and comments); **Messages** pushes an announcement to members or keeps a group conversation alive. On-screen labels are in French, so they are quoted as they appear.

## Steps

1. **Actualités** → **Nouvel article**: **Titre \***, **Contenu \***, and an **URL image (optionnel)** — the image is not uploaded here, you paste a link. **Publier** puts the article online at once: there is no draft and no scheduled publishing. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/actualites-et-messages/1.png" alt="New article modal" />
2. Each article card shows its date, an excerpt, its number of likes and comments — your athletes' reactions in the app. The newspaper icon reopens the article in **Modifier**, the bin deletes it after a confirmation.
3. **Messages** → **Envoyer une annonce** (at the bottom of the **Conversations** column) for a notification: pick a **Type** (**Annonce**, **Rappel**, **Alerte**, **Info**), a **Titre (optionnel)**, the **Message \***, and the **Groupe cible** — **Tous les membres** by default, or one of your groups. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/actualites-et-messages/2.png" alt="Send a message form" />
4. **Envoyer** broadcasts in the back-office and in the app at the same time: the member gets the announcement in real time. A sent announcement cannot be edited nor withdrawn.
5. To chat, select a group in the **Conversations** column: the thread is grouped by day, your messages on the right, members' messages on the left with their username. Until you have a group, the column offers **Créer un groupe**.
6. Targeting announcements and conversations relies on your groups: **Groupes** is where you choose who belongs to them.

<Callout>
A news item is content that stays and can be commented on; an announcement is a notification that goes by. For a schedule change this week, prefer the announcement; to present an event, the news item.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **The Publier button stays greyed out.** **Titre** and **Contenu** are both required.
- **The image does not show.** The field expects a public **URL** (`https://…`): a file from your computer will not work.
- **An announcement never reaches members.** It was targeted at a group: members outside that group do not receive it.
- **You cannot find where to fix an announcement.** There is no editing: send a new one.
- **The conversation list is empty.** No group exists yet → **Groupes** → **Nouveau groupe**.

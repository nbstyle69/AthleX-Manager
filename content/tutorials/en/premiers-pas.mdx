## Before you start

You have just created your box. The Dashboard is the back-office home page, and the sidebar groups sections by role (training, community, events, steering). Allow five minutes for this setup. On-screen labels are in French, so they are quoted as they appear.

## Steps

1. Open the **Dashboard**: the counters at the top (**Tournois actifs**, **Membres**, **Scores en attente**, **Messages non lus**: active tournaments, members, pending scores, unread messages) are shortcuts, not just numbers — clicking one opens the matching section. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/premiers-pas/1.png" alt="Dashboard: the four box counters and the invitation code" />
2. Go to **Réglages** (Settings) and fill in **Informations de la box** (name, description, address, contact), then add your **Logo de la box** and your **Bannière de la box** (banner). These are what your members and public page visitors see. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/premiers-pas/2.png" alt="Top of the Informations de la box block in Settings" />
3. In **Membres**, switch your coaches to **Coach** from the **Rôle** column; they then show up in **Réglages** → **Détails de la box**. A coach sees the Whiteboard, schedules, schedule templates, messages and the Help tab; a coach never sees the Dashboard, prices or subscribers. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/premiers-pas/5.png" alt="Coaches listed in Détails de la box, in Settings" />
4. Copy the **Code d'invitation box** (invitation code) from the Dashboard (**Copier le code** button) and send it to your members: that is how they join the box in the athlete app. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/premiers-pas/6.png" alt="Box invite code on the dashboard" />
5. Create your first groups (Groupes) if you program differently per time slot or level: groups are then used to target WODs. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/premiers-pas/7.png" alt="Groups page with a Semaine group and a Jour par jour group" />
6. Decide how members come in: a trial from your public page, a personal invitation, an online subscription, or the box code. The four journeys are compared in « Les quatre façons d'entrer dans ta box » (the four ways into your box), and the personal link with a pre-assigned plan in « Inviter un membre nominativement ».

## Need help?

The sidebar has a **Support** entry: « Une question, un bug ou une idée pour AthleX Manager ? Contactez l'équipe AthleX. » (a question, a bug or an idea? contact the AthleX team).

1. **Nouvelle demande** (new request), then pick the type: **Question**, **Bug** or **Idée d'amélioration** (improvement idea). <Screenshot src="/tutorials/premiers-pas/4.png" alt="New support request form" />
2. Give a short **Titre de la demande** (title) and describe the request: the page involved, what you did, what you expected, what happened, and the member or plan concerned. That is what avoids a round trip.
3. **Envoyer** (send) creates the ticket with status **Ouvert** (open). It becomes **Répondu** (answered) once the team replies, then **Résolu** (resolved) — click **Marquer résolu** in the ticket once it is sorted; a resolved ticket cannot be reopened.
4. A badge on the **Support** entry flags unread replies: reopen the ticket to read the thread and answer inside it.

## Common mistakes

- **A coach says the plans page and the Marketplace are missing.** That is intended: those pages set prices and are owner-only → use your own owner account.
- **A member cannot find the box in the app.** The invitation code was never sent, or was retyped with a typo → send it again from the Dashboard.
- **The logo does not show on the athlete side.** The image was not saved in Réglages, or the upload failed → reload the page and check that the logo preview appears.

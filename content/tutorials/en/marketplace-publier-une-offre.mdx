## Before you start

Publishing an offer means proposing your programming to other boxes. The **Mes offres** tab of the Marketplace (`/programming/offers`) holds your published and draft programmings.

## Steps

1. Open **Marketplace → Mes offres** (`/programming/offers`) and click **Nouvelle programmation**: **Titre**, **Description**, **Objectif**, **Public visé**, **Matériel**, **Discipline**, **Niveau**, **Jours / semaine**, **Nombre de semaines** and **Facturation** (Gratuit, Paiement unique or Mensuel, with its **Prix (€)**), then **Créer et ajouter des WOD**. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/marketplace-publier-une-offre/1.png" alt="Nouvelle programmation window: title, description, goal, target audience, equipment, discipline, level, weeks, billing" />
2. Fill in its weeks. The fastest route: from the Whiteboard, display a week that worked and click **Copier vers une offre**, then pick the offer and the week number. You can also do it in the offer window: pick the **Semaine**, then **Ajouter** a day (Lun…Dim), or **Importer (CSV, JSON, PDF)**.
3. Check the content week by week: every empty week is shown in orange, and all of them need at least one WOD before publishing.
4. Click **Publier** on the offer row: it becomes **Publiée**, joins the **Catalogue** and becomes visible to other boxes. **Dépublier** puts it back to **Brouillon**.
5. If you run several boxes, the offer is published on behalf of the active box (selector at the top of the sidebar); none of your boxes sees it in its catalogue.

## Common mistakes

- **"Publication refusée : …" (publishing refused).** An offer without description, goal or target audience, or with a week without any WOD, cannot be published, and an empty offer never appears in the catalogue: the message says what is missing → complete it, then click **Publier** again.
- **"I saved a week template and the offer stayed empty."** A week template is internal to your box: it does not populate an offer → use **Copier vers une offre**.
- **Subscribed boxes do not see my correction.** Copies are independent: editing an offer week does not rewrite what has already been received → warn your subscribers, or fix the next week.
- **My offer is missing from my own catalogue.** That is intended: you cannot subscribe to your own offers → review it from the "Mes offres" tab.

## Before you start

Publishing an offer means proposing your programming to other boxes. The **Mes offres** tab of the Marketplace (`/programming/offers`) holds your published and draft programmings.

## Steps

1. Open **Marketplace → Mes offres** (`/programming/offers`) and create a programming: title, description, goal, target audience, discipline, level, WODs per week and billing. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/marketplace-publier-une-offre/1.png" alt="My offers tab of the Marketplace" />
2. Fill in its weeks. The fastest route: from the Whiteboard, display a week that worked and click **Copier vers une offre**, then pick the offer and the week number.
3. Check the content week by week. The screen flags an offer whose week 1 is empty.
4. Click the publish control on the offer: it joins the **Catalogue** and becomes visible to other boxes.
5. If you run several boxes, you can also broadcast it to your own boxes, unticking those you want to exclude.

## Common mistakes

- **"Publication refusée" (publishing refused).** An offer with no WOD cannot be published and never appears in the catalogue → fill at least week 1, then publish again.
- **"I saved a week template and the offer stayed empty."** A week template is internal to your box: it does not populate an offer → use **Copier vers une offre**.
- **Subscribed boxes do not see my correction.** Copies are independent: editing an offer week does not rewrite what has already been received → warn your subscribers, or fix the next week.
- **My offer is missing from my own catalogue.** That is intended: you cannot subscribe to your own offers → review it from the "Mes offres" tab.

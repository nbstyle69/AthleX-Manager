## Before you start

The **Dashboard** is the first menu entry. It gathers your counters, your gym's invite code, its logo, and the latest tournaments and scores. **Support**, pinned at the bottom of the menu above **Réglages**, puts you in touch with the AthleX team. Both screens are for the owner and co-owner only: a coach who opens the Dashboard is sent to the **Whiteboard**, and **Support** answers them « Accès refusé ». On-screen labels are in French, so they are quoted as they appear.

## Steps: the Dashboard

1. Read the four tiles at the top: **Tournois actifs** (active tournaments), **Membres** (active memberships), **Scores en attente** (pending scores) and **Messages non lus** (messages from others in your groups since your last visit to **Messages**). Each tile is a link: **Tournois actifs** and **Scores en attente** open **Tournois**, the other two open **Membres** and **Messages**.
2. The **Code d'invitation box** block shows the code your athletes type in the AthleX app to enter your gym. Three icons on the right: **Copier le code** (a green tick confirms the copy), **Générer un nouveau code** (new code) and **Modifier manuellement** (edit by hand).
3. To pick your own code, click **Modifier manuellement**, type it in the **Nouveau code…** field (3 to 12 characters, turned into capitals), then **Sauvegarder**. The cross cancels. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/dashboard-code-d-invitation-et-support/1.png" alt="Code d'invitation box block in edit mode, with the message “Ce code est déjà utilisé par une autre box. Choisissez-en un autre.”" />
4. Below the code, **Logo de la box**: **Uploader** (or **Changer** if there is one already) and **Supprimer**. Square, at least 512×512 px, 2 MB at most. « Logo mis à jour ! » confirms the upload. It is the same logo as in **Réglages**.
5. At the bottom, **Tournois récents** lists the last three non-archived tournaments with their participants and status, and **Scores à valider** the last five pending scores. Click a row to open the tournament or its scores page: validation happens there, the ✓ and ✗ dots on the Dashboard are not buttons. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/dashboard-code-d-invitation-et-support/2.png" alt="Tournois récents and Scores à valider blocks of the Dashboard, with four pending scores" />

<Callout type="warning">
**Générer un nouveau code** runs without confirmation: the old code stops working at once. Tell your athletes if you had displayed or shared it.
</Callout>

## Steps: writing to support

1. Open **Support**. The list shows your requests, newest first: type, subject, date and status **Ouvert** (open), **Répondu** (answered) or **Résolu** (resolved). A dot next to the subject flags a reply you have not read yet, and the **Support** menu entry shows their number. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/dashboard-code-d-invitation-et-support/3.png" alt="Support page: three requests, one of them “Répondu” and unread" />
2. Click **Nouvelle demande**, choose **Question**, **Bug** or **Idée d'amélioration** (improvement idea), fill in **Titre de la demande** and **Décrivez votre demande...**, then **Envoyer**.
3. Click a request to open its thread: your messages (**Vous**) on the right, those from **Support AthleX** on the left. Opening the thread marks the reply as read. Reply in **Votre message...** then with the send arrow.
4. Once it is sorted, click **Marquer résolu** (no confirmation). The reply field disappears. **Retour aux demandes** takes you back to the list. <Screenshot src="/tutorials/dashboard-code-d-invitation-et-support/4.png" alt="Request thread with the Support AthleX reply and the Marquer résolu button" />

## Common mistakes

- **« Ce code est déjà utilisé par une autre box. Choisissez-en un autre. »** Codes are unique across AthleX → pick another one.
- **« Le code doit contenir au moins 3 caractères »** or **Sauvegarder** greyed out. Your code is too short → 3 characters at least.
- **An « Archivage programmé » or « Box archivée » window: « Cette box n'accepte plus de nouveaux membres. »** Your gym is being archived: the code can no longer change (see “AthleX subscription and gym archiving”).
- **« Erreur réseau ».** The connection dropped → try again.
- **You can no longer reply to a request.** It is **Résolu** → open a **Nouvelle demande**.
- **You click Envoyer and your text stays on screen.** Sending failed, with no error message → try again a little later; your text is not lost as long as you stay on the page.
